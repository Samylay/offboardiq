# OffboardIQ — Go-to-Market Strategy

## Positioning Statement

**For mid-market companies (100-2,000 employees) who lose critical institutional knowledge every time an employee leaves, OffboardIQ is the AI-powered knowledge capture platform that transforms the post-resignation window from a knowledge-loss crisis into a knowledge-preservation opportunity. Unlike generic HR offboarding tools that focus on compliance checklists, OffboardIQ uses structured AI interviews to extract, organize, and transfer the tacit knowledge that would otherwise walk out the door.**

## Market Entry Strategy

### Phase 1: Founder-Led Sales (Months 1-6)
**Goal**: 20 paying customers, $50k ARR

- Target: Engineering-heavy companies with 100-500 employees
- Channel: Direct outreach to VP of Engineering / Head of People
- Hook: "Your last departure cost you 3 months of ramp-up time. What if you could cut that to 3 weeks?"
- Qualification: Have they lost someone critical in the last 6 months?
- Entry point: Free pilot on next departure

**Why engineering-heavy first:**
- Highest pain (engineers hold deep technical knowledge)
- Easiest to quantify loss (production incidents, delayed features)
- Champions are technically savvy (appreciate AI approach)
- Fastest word-of-mouth in tight-knit communities

### Phase 2: Content & Community (Months 4-12)
**Goal**: Inbound pipeline, 100 customers, $300k ARR

Content pillars:
1. **"The Knowledge Debt Calculator"** — Interactive tool that estimates the cost of knowledge loss based on company size, turnover rate, and average tenure
2. **"Offboarding Playbook"** — Free comprehensive guide to structured offboarding (establishes thought leadership, captures emails)
3. **Monthly "Knowledge Risk Report"** — Industry benchmarks on knowledge retention practices
4. **Case studies** — Quantified impact stories from early customers

Distribution:
- LinkedIn (HR tech + engineering leadership audience)
- HR tech newsletters (People Managing People, HR Brew)
- Engineering blogs (cross-post on HackerNews, dev.to)
- HR tech conference circuit (HR Tech, SHRM, Unleash)

### Phase 3: Channel Partnerships (Months 9-18)
**Goal**: Partner-driven pipeline, 500 customers, $1.5M ARR

Priority partnerships:
1. **HRIS integrations** — BambooHR marketplace, Rippling app directory, Gusto ecosystem
2. **HR consulting firms** — White-label or referral partnerships
3. **IT consulting firms** — Include in offboarding methodology engagements
4. **Recruiting firms** — Natural touchpoint (they see the resignations first)

### Phase 4: Product-Led Growth (Months 12-24)
**Goal**: Self-serve motion, 2,000+ customers, $5M ARR

- Free tier: 2 departures/year with basic AI interviews
- In-product upgrade triggers: hitting departure limit, needing advanced risk scoring
- Viral loop: Knowledge base items shared with successors create new user accounts
- Department-level expansion: Engineering buys → Product adopts → CS follows

## Pricing Strategy

### Revised Pricing (Iteration 3 refinement)

**Insight from market analysis**: Per-seat pricing penalizes growth. Per-departure pricing aligns cost with value but creates unpredictable revenue. Solution: hybrid model.

| Plan | Monthly Platform Fee | Per-Departure Fee | Best For |
|------|---------------------|-------------------|----------|
| **Starter** | $0 | $99/departure | < 10 departures/year, testing the tool |
| **Growth** | $299/month | $149/departure | 10-50 departures/year, mid-market |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | 50+/year, SSO, API, compliance |

**Annual commitment**: 20% discount on platform fee + 15% off per-departure

**Why this works**:
- Starter: Low barrier to entry, no commitment needed
- Growth: Platform fee covers proactive features (risk map, knowledge base) that deliver value even without active departures
- Enterprise: Custom deals for high-volume, high-complexity needs
- The per-departure model means revenue scales with the problem (more departures = more value = more revenue)

### Revenue math (Growth plan, typical mid-market):
- 200-employee company, ~15% annual turnover = ~30 departures/year
- Revenue: ($299 × 12) + ($149 × 30) = $3,588 + $4,470 = **$8,058/year**
- At 1,000 customers: **$8M ARR**

## Key Sales Objections & Responses

### "We already have an offboarding process"
*"Great — how much of it captures the undocumented knowledge in your departing employees' heads? The procedures they don't even realize they know? We're not replacing your process — we're adding the knowledge capture layer that your checklist can't do."*

### "We use Confluence/Notion for knowledge"
*"Those tools assume knowledge is already documented. The problem is the 80% of critical operational knowledge that never makes it into a wiki. OffboardIQ actively extracts that knowledge through structured AI interviews."*

### "Can't we just do exit interviews?"
*"Exit interviews capture sentiment — why someone left. They don't capture the 47 undocumented procedures, the vendor contacts, the 'here's why that API is configured this way' context. That's what OffboardIQ extracts."*

### "This seems like a point solution"
*"It starts as departure-triggered, but the knowledge base and risk map become permanent organizational assets. Companies use our proactive risk map year-round, not just during departures. We're a knowledge resilience platform, not just an offboarding tool."*

### "AI can't really do this effectively"
*"Our 5-phase interview methodology is designed by knowledge management experts and refined across hundreds of departure interviews. The AI doesn't just ask questions — it follows up on vague answers, probes for tacit knowledge, and extracts structured data. We typically capture 3-5x more knowledge items than manual interviews."*

## Competitive Response Playbook

### If BambooHR adds AI offboarding
- Emphasize depth vs. breadth ("they bolted on a feature; we built the platform")
- Our 5-phase methodology vs. their generic chatbot
- Proactive risk mapping (they'll never do this — not in their product DNA)

### If a new VC-funded competitor emerges
- Speed to market advantage + established customer relationships
- Patent/IP on interview methodology (file provisional early)
- Knowledge base network effects create switching costs

### If Guru/Notion adds offboarding workflows
- They optimize for ongoing documentation, not departure-triggered extraction
- No risk scoring, no proactive analysis
- Different buyer (knowledge management vs. HR)
